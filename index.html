Hashi

Hashi: BridgesOn Google Play

The Japanese logic puzzle, also called Hashiwokakero or simply Bridges. Join the islands with bridges until every island has the number it asks for and the whole board is one network. Four sizes from 7×7 to 15×15, a hundred levels each to learn on, then Hard and Expert that never run out, plus a daily puzzle and a weekly tournament. Hints when you want them, a check when you are stuck, and nineteen languages.

Never a guess: every board is generated with exactly one solution and then checked to be solvable by reasoning alone. Plays with no connection.

Free to play, paid for by ads. Your progress follows you to a new phone.

Nothing to install to try it: the browser version plays on a phone or a desktop, and can be kept on your home screen without going near a store.
