Hashi

Hashi — BridgesSoon on Play

The Japanese logic puzzle: join the islands with bridges until every island has the number it asks for and the whole board is one network. Four sizes from 7×7 to 15×15 — a hundred levels each to learn on, then Hard and Expert that never run out — plus a daily puzzle and a weekly tournament. Hints when you want them, a check when you are stuck, and twelve languages.

Free to play, paid for by ads. Your progress follows you to a new phone.
