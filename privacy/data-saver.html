Data Saver

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Applies to the Android app Data Saver: Usage & Blocker, package com.anubisproductions.datagate, published by Anubis Productions, Cairo, Egypt.

The short version

The app has no internet permission. Not "we promise not to use it": Android will not let it open a network connection at all, because the permission is absent from the app. Nothing it reads about your phone can be sent anywhere, by us or by anyone else.

What the app reads, and why

  • Per-app data usage, through Android's usage access, which you grant by hand in system settings. This is what the app is for: showing which apps spent your mobile data.
  • Which apps are installed, limited to what Android's scoped package visibility allows, so the list can show names and icons.
  • Connectivity state, to tell mobile data from Wi-Fi. This reports the state of the connection; it does not grant access to it.
  • A local VPN service, which is how Android lets an app block another app's traffic on the device. It is a loopback with no server: nothing is routed anywhere, and there is nothing to route it with.

All of it stays in the app's own storage on your phone.

What the app does not do

  • No internet access, so no servers, no accounts, no sync, no backups.
  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Nothing is collected, sold or shared, because nothing can leave the device.

Notifications

The app can show a notification about its running service. Notifications stay on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings. There is no copy anywhere else to delete, and nothing for us to look up.

Source

The app is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence, so the claims above can be checked rather than taken on trust: source on GitHub.

Changes
