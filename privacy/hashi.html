Hashi

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Applies to the Android game Hashi, package com.anubisproductions.hashi, published by Anubis Productions, Cairo, Egypt.

In short

Hashi is free to play and paid for by advertising, with a one-off purchase that switches the ads off. It saves your progress to our server so it survives a new phone and so the weekly tournament can be ranked. It never asks for your name or your email address, and there is no sign-up.

What we store about you

When the game first runs it signs in anonymously with Google Firebase, which issues a random identifier for that installation. It is not your Google account and not your device's advertising identifier. Everything we keep is filed against that identifier:

  • your progress: levels solved, stars, best times, coins, streak, achievements;
  • the puzzle you are in the middle of;
  • your weekly tournament score, with a display name the game invents from the identifier, such as "Builder 7F3A";
  • whether you have bought the ad-free upgrade.

Your settings and a copy of the same progress are also held on the phone itself, and uninstalling removes that copy.

Who else sees data

  • Google Firebase stores the records above on our behalf, in Google's European region, and sees the technical details any internet request carries, such as your IP address. See the Firebase privacy documentation.
  • Google AdMob supplies the advertising. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to choose and measure ads, as described in how Google uses data from apps that use its advertising services. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before requesting a personalised ad, and you can change that choice later from the game's settings.
  • Google Play handles the ad-free purchase. We never see your payment details; Play tells the game only that the purchase exists.

Nobody else. We do not sell data, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

What the game does not collect

  • No name, email address or account of any kind.
  • No contacts, photos, files, location, microphone or camera. The only Android permission the game asks for is internet access.
  • No analytics or crash reporting beyond what Firebase and AdMob need to run.

Children

Hashi is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Write to anubisproductionsmarketing@gmail.com and ask. Since nothing we hold carries your name, quote the display name the tournament shows you so we can find the record. Uninstalling the game removes the copy on your phone and stops anything further being stored.

Changes

If this policy changes the date at the top changes with it, and the earlier wording stays in the history of the public repository that serves this page.
