Hashi

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Applies to the Android app Hashi, package com.anubisproductions.hashi, published by Anubis Productions, Cairo, Egypt.

The short version

Hashi does not ask who you are. There is no sign-up, no email address and no name. The puzzles run on your phone, and the game works with the network turned off. What does leave the phone is your game progress, attached to a random identifier, so it survives a reinstall and so the weekly tournament can show a ranking.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your progress: levels solved, stars, best times, coins, streak, achievements.
  • Your settings: language, sound, music, high contrast.
  • The puzzle you are in the middle of, so you can come back to it.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

What leaves your phone

The game signs in anonymously to Google Firebase. Anonymous means Firebase issues a random identifier for the installation; it is not linked to your Google account, your email or your device's advertising identifier. Against that identifier the game stores:

  • a copy of the progress listed above, so a new phone can pick it up;
  • your weekly tournament score, and a display name the game makes up from the identifier, such as "Builder 7F3A". You are not asked for a name and none is read from your device.

Google, as the operator of Firebase, also sees the technical details any internet request carries, such as your IP address. Google's handling of that is covered by the Firebase privacy documentation. Data is held in Google's European region.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising network is built into this version of the game. If one is added, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and users in regions that require consent will be asked before any personalised ad is requested.
  • No analytics or crash reporting service.
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera. The app requests only internet access.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

Children

Hashi is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Having your data deleted

Write to anubisproductionsmarketing@gmail.com from any address and ask. The only thing we hold is the stored copy of a game record against a random identifier, so to find yours we need that identifier: open Settings in the game, where the version line is, and quote the display name the tournament shows you. Deleting the app also stops anything further being stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous wording stays in the page's history in the public repository that serves this site.
