The rules, in four lines

  • Every island shows how many bridges must reach it.
  • Bridges run straight across or straight down, never diagonally.
  • Bridges never cross, and at most two join the same pair.
  • When you are done, every island can be reached from every other one.

This board needs no guessing

Every bridge on it can be worked out with counting, crossing and the isolation rule. It was generated and then handed to a solver that is only allowed to reason. Any board that solver cannot finish is thrown away, so if you are stuck here there is a move you have not spotted yet, never a coin to flip.

This one takes 34 counting steps, 1 crossing steps and 4 isolation steps.
